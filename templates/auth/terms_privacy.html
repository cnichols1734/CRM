Privacy Policy

1. Introduction

Origen Technolog operates the AgentFlow web CRM, the AgentFlow agent app and the ClientFlow client app. This policy explains the information we use to provide these services, who receives it, and how to request access, correction or deletion.

A brokerage using our services also maintains its own client and transaction records. Its privacy and recordkeeping obligations apply to those records. We help process requests involving data held in our services.

2. Information we collect

  • Accounts: Name, email, password credentials, and any phone, brokerage, license or professional profile details you provide. Passwords are stored as hashes.
  • ClientFlow: Saved homes, search requests, inquiries, tour requests, messages, and the account or invitation details used to connect eligible deals. The app also stores preferences, cached content and move-planning estimates on your device.
  • AgentFlow and CRM: Contacts, tasks, notes, transaction details, messages, property information, documents and attachments that you or authorized participants provide. Transaction documents may include financial or other personal information.
  • BOB and transcription: Prompts, relevant CRM context, attachments, voice audio and transcripts you choose to submit to the AI features. Agent voice memos can create transcripts saved with contacts.
  • Device and service information: IP addresses, request and error logs, session identifiers, app preferences and device push tokens. Web analytics can record page visits and product actions. Listing searches send the search criteria needed to retrieve results.
  • Connected services: When you connect email or calendar services, we use authorization tokens and the information needed for the features you enable.

ClientFlow maps show property locations. The current native app does not request your device's precise location. The apps do not collect payment card details through an in-app purchase flow.

3. How we use information

We use information to create accounts, verify access, save your choices, connect you with your brokerage, manage contacts and transactions, deliver messages and documents, provide requested AI features, and send notifications or calendar updates you enable.

We also use service logs and product analytics to diagnose errors, improve the service, prevent abuse and protect accounts. We use contact details to respond to requests and send service or security notices. Marketing messages include an unsubscribe option.

4. Sharing and service providers

We do not sell your personal information.

Brokerages and participants: Your selected brokerage or agent receives the inquiries and messages you send. Deal information and documents are available to authorized people in that transaction. Creating a ClientFlow account does not grant access to every brokerage record.

Service providers: Railway hosts the backend; Supabase provides database and file storage services; SendGrid delivers email; DocuSeal supports document signing; and Repliers supplies listing results and listing images. Apple services provide native maps and push notification delivery. Configured web analytics and monitoring include PostHog and New Relic. Each receives the information needed for its role. Listing image requests can expose your IP address to the image provider.

OpenAI: AgentFlow's BOB chat and voice features send your prompts, selected attachments, relevant CRM context and voice audio to OpenAI to generate responses or transcripts. BOB asks for data-sharing permission before sending this information. You can reset that permission in Account. Contact voice memos ask separately before recording and transcription. Resetting permission does not delete earlier conversations or information already submitted. ClientFlow does not include BOB.

Optional integrations: Connecting Google email or calendar services allows the enabled integration to exchange the relevant email, calendar and account information. Opening an external signing, listing or other service link is also subject to that provider's privacy policy.

We may disclose information when required by law or to protect rights and safety. A business transfer may require transferring service records subject to applicable privacy obligations. We request consent where required for other sharing.

5. Data security

The apps connect to the service over HTTPS. We use password hashing, authenticated sessions and account, organization and transaction access checks. Credentials and session tokens should not be shared with other people.

No system can guarantee absolute security. Contact us if you believe someone has accessed your account without permission.

Depending on your location, you may have the following rights:

  • Access: Request a copy of the personal information we hold about you
  • Correction: Update or correct inaccurate personal information
  • Deletion: Request deletion of your personal information (subject to legal obligations)
  • Portability: Export your data in a machine-readable format
  • Opt-Out: Unsubscribe from marketing communications (service emails still required)
  • Restriction: Request limitation of processing in certain circumstances
  • Objection: Object to processing based on legitimate interests

GDPR (European Users): If you are located in the European Economic Area, you have additional rights under GDPR, including the right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority.

CCPA (California Users): California residents have rights under the California Consumer Privacy Act, including the right to know, delete, and opt-out of the sale of personal information (note: we do not sell personal information).

Exercising Your Rights: To exercise any of these rights, please contact us using the information provided at the end of this policy. We will respond within 30 days.

7. Local storage, permissions and preferences

The website uses cookies for sign-in and preferences and may use configured analytics. You can manage cookies in your browser; blocking sign-in cookies can prevent access.

The native apps use local storage for preferences, cached content and saved app state, and Keychain storage for account credentials such as session tokens. Some local content remains available when offline.

AgentFlow asks for microphone access before recording audio. You can decline or change microphone and notification permissions in iOS Settings. ClientFlow calendar subscriptions are optional; anyone with a subscription URL may be able to read its dates, so keep the link private. Disable the link in the app and remove the subscription from your calendar app when you no longer want it.

The current native apps do not use advertising identifiers or cross-app advertising tracking. Web browser Do Not Track signals do not change the service's processing.

8. Children's Privacy

The Service is not intended for individuals under the age of 18. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe we have collected information from a child, please contact us immediately, and we will delete it.

9. International Data Transfers

Your information may be transferred to and processed in countries other than your country of residence. These countries may have data protection laws different from your jurisdiction. By using the Service, you consent to such transfers.

When transferring data internationally, we implement appropriate safeguards such as Standard Contractual Clauses and ensure service providers comply with applicable data protection laws.

10. Retention and account deletion

We retain account information while needed to provide the service. You can initiate deletion from within either app:

  • AgentFlow: Open Account > Delete account and confirm with your password. We save the request and show its deadline in the app. Christopher Nichols handles requests. We complete deletion within 30 days and email confirmation. You can still sign in while the request is processed.
  • ClientFlow: Open Settings > Your account > Delete account and confirm with your password. This removes your browsing account, saved homes, inquiries and inquiry messages, and invalidates deal sessions linked to that account.

Deleting a ClientFlow browsing account does not automatically delete your brokerage's contacts, shared transaction documents or other participants' records. Contact us to request deletion of your personal information in those records. We review ownership and applicable legal retention obligations rather than deleting other people's records. A separate invitation to a deal is a separate access grant.

For retained information, we identify the category, legal basis and retention period when responding to your request. We remove information that no longer needs to be retained. Sign-out or a deletion-request receipt does not mean deletion has finished.

Backup copies may persist for up to 90 days under our existing backup retention policy. Information subject to a legal hold is retained as required. Completion records are limited to what is needed to document fulfillment and any retention obligations.

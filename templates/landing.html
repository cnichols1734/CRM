What AgentFlow does

The client record is just the start

Your contacts are only part of the job. AgentFlow keeps the notes, follow-ups, tasks, and activity around each client in the same place. You can manage it yourself when you want to, or tell B.O.B. what needs to happen.

B.

Tell B.O.B. what you need done

B.O.B. is the AI assistant built into AgentFlow. Ask it about what's in your CRM or tell it to take care of something for you.

Find a client. Update their information. Add a note. Log a call. Finish a task. Organize contacts. Those are just examples.

If you can do it in AgentFlow, you can ask B.O.B. to do it for you.

You can also connect B.O.B. to Telegram and message it when you're away from AgentFlow. A voice note or a photo of a business card works too.

  • Works with your AgentFlow data
  • Takes actions inside the CRM
  • 25 messages a day on the free plan

Common questions

What does the free plan include?

One user, up to 10,000 contacts, tasks, and a dashboard. You can send email through Gmail and sync tasks to Google Calendar. B.O.B. is included, with 25 messages a day.

Do I need a credit card?

No. You can start without one. What's on the free plan stays free. Extra features later will be paid. Nothing paid is for sale today.

How long does it take to get started?

Setup takes about two minutes.

Is this built for real estate agents?

Yes. Contacts, follow-ups, and tasks are set up the way agents actually work, not as a generic CRM.

What can B.O.B. do?

B.O.B. is the AI assistant built into AgentFlow. Instead of clicking through the CRM, just tell B.O.B. what you need done. It can find and update contacts, manage tasks, log activity, organize clients, and much more. If you can do it in AgentFlow, you can ask B.O.B. to do it for you.

The free plan includes 25 messages a day, and you can also chat with B.O.B. through Telegram after connecting it from your profile.
